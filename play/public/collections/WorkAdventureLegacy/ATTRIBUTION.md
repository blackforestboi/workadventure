# WorkAdventure legacy map assets

The images in `assets/` were recovered unchanged from the historical WorkAdventure map directories `maps/Floor0`, `maps/Floor1`, `maps/Floor2`, and `maps/Lyon` immediately before the starter-kit migration on 2021-09-23.

They retain the project's original base-asset attribution and licensing terms:

- CC BY-SA 3.0 — <http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>
- GNU GPL 3.0 — <http://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html>
- Source attribution: `hello@workadventu.re`

The original attribution record specifically identifies the base tilesets, `walls2.png`, and related map artwork.
Do not remove this file when redistributing or modifying the legacy collection.
